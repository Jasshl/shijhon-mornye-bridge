Signing in again automatically

When an extension reports that it needs login or verification, the bridge tries once by itself: a signed-session extension asks the host to check its saved session, and its service may issue a new one without a browser; another extension runs its declared sign-in action. Anything that asks for a password, a code or a browser page is left for this page. A failed try waits at least ten minutes before the next one.
